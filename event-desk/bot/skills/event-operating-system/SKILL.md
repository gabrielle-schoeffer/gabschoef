# Event operating system

Gabrielle executes and wants the why before the task. Ally owns the motion, reviews copy, and is the partner on the brief. They work in Slack day to day. This desk drafts. It does not send.

## Copy

Draft one reusable cadence. Do not invent a new sequence per event. Swap the event, topic, and speaker, and mark those tokens so an AE can see them: **[Event]**, **[Topic]**, **[Speaker]**.

Two audiences, always:

- Net-new. Cold enough that the note has to earn the reply.
- In-funnel or customer. Warmer. The ask is a hold on the calendar, not an introduction.

Two situations:

- Met onsite. Soft intro that the team connected at the event, name the account owner, offer the next community moment. Ask "would you want to join?" Do not hard-ask for a date.
- Did not meet. Say so plainly, offer 15 minutes, reference a session or roundtable, and add one value line about what marketing leaders are saying. Do not say "we sponsored."

Voice: a person writing to one person. Short. No brochure language. Subject lines are specific to the event, not "Quick question."

If `listSource` is `vendor`, the first email also says they opted in to hear from sponsors of the relevant category when they registered for the named conference, links the privacy policy, and includes unsubscribe. Ally reviews that draft before it is shown as ready. If `listSource` is `unknown`, do not draft sendable copy. Ask.

Overlapping lists: one person is in one sequence. Say which message wins and stop.

## Handoff

Use the `handoff` object from `build_workback`. Tell Gabrielle to send Ben the segment, the Lemlist campaign, the sender rule, and the tracker link only after the segment is ICP-only and Ally has reviewed the copy.

Arthur gets one action per message. Create the marketing event, wait for confirmation, then upload. Registered and attended are explicit. A side event is its own marketing event under the parent campaign.

## KBYG

One page, built by T-3 days: date and venue, travel, hotel, what is shipping and where, the agenda with AirOps moments marked, booth or room, and day-of phone numbers. Ally is event lead. Alex and Matt host executives. Jess owns capture and brand. Each AE has three confirmed conversations and three to five named people.

## Retro

Within two weeks: did the job happen, what percent of the room was target accounts, what to change in the six-touch sequence, what to cut next time. Write the answer as notes they can paste into the event workbook. Do not turn it into a new strategy deck.
